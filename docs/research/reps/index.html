Relational–Event Packaging across Northern Eurasia and the Indigenous Americas

REPS · Working paper / hypothesis paper

Abstract. This project compares recurring relational and event-packaging patterns across selected languages of Northern Eurasia and the Indigenous Americas. The REPS model is presented as a falsifiable typological hypothesis, not as proof of a single genealogical language family.

Keywords: linguistic typology; relational encoding; event packaging; Koreanic; Japonic; Mongolic; Turkic; Tungusic; Indigenous American languages; Northern Eurasia.
