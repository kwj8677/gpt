## Core thesis
The strongest explanation is not “Korea punished thieves harder.” It is a compound incentive shift: **less anonymous cash + more traceability + tighter linkage from incident to identity + higher perceived certainty of apprehension**. Italy has substantial policing, but its tourist-zone exposure and post-2022 procedural frictions have historically weakened the same certainty loop; 2023 and 2026 legal changes show an ongoing correction.

## Editorial standard
Facts, mechanism-level inference and uncertainty are separated. Korean incident counts and Italian victimisation rates are not presented as directly comparable country rankings.
