## Core thesis
The strongest explanation is not “one country punishes thieves harder.” Across high-safety systems the recurring output is **less anonymous loot + more traceability + faster linkage from incident to identity + higher perceived certainty of apprehension**. Seoul is the most legible before/after case in this package; Japan and Singapore serve as comparison systems; Italy shows how tourist exposure and procedural friction can weaken the same certainty loop.

## Editorial standard
Facts, mechanism-level inference and uncertainty are separated. Korean incident counts and Italian victimisation rates are not presented as directly comparable country rankings.
